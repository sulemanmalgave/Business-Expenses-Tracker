## 3. Full Description
**Business Expense Tracker – Small Business** is an offline-first financial management and bookkeeping application built specifically for freelancers, consultants, contractors, and small business owners.

Unlike cloud accounting software that charges high recurring subscriptions and hosts your confidential financial data on remote servers, Business Expense Tracker keeps 100% of your records safely on your own Windows device.

### Key Capabilities:
- **Financial Dashboard**: Monitor Total Income, Total Expenses, Net Cash Flow, and operating margins with decimal-safe financial calculations.
- **Dynamic Period Filters**: View finances by This Month, Last Month, Financial Year (April–March or January–December), All Time, or custom date ranges.
- **Client Invoices & Estimates**: Generate professional invoices with customizable tax/GST rates, itemized line items, print/PDF previews, and one-click income ledger recording.
- **Account Transfer Isolation**: Transfers between checking, savings, and cash accounts are recorded accurately without falsifying income or expense metrics.
- **Flexible Categorization**: Categorize overhead costs and income streams with custom color-coded categories, client tags, and project tags.
- **Project & Client Profitability**: Track net revenue contributions per client or project without double-counting.
- **Scheduled Obligations & Cash Flow Forecasting**: Set recurring reminders for subscriptions, rent, and retainers. Preview projected 30-day and 60-day cash outflows to avoid cash surprises.
- **Receipt & Attachment Ingest**: Attach digital receipts (PDF, PNG, JPG, WebP) directly to transactions with client-side heuristic metadata extraction.
- **Free, Unrestricted Data Export**: Export standard bookkeeping CSV or Excel-compatible spreadsheets without paywalls.
- **Offline Data Ownership**: Uses browser-grade IndexedDB storage on your device. Complete JSON backup and restore with duplicate detection.
- **Indian & Global Localization**: Supports Indian Rupee (₹) with Lakhs/Crores numbering (`1,50,000.00`), standard USD ($), GBP (£), EUR (€), and customizable fiscal year start.

## 5. Privacy Policy & Disclosures
- **Privacy Policy URL**: `https://com.businessexpensetracker.app/privacy` (also accessible directly within app under Settings > Privacy Policy)
- **Data Collection**: None. The application operates 100% on the user's local device (IndexedDB). No financial data, receipts, invoices, or customer details are transmitted to external servers.
- **Third-Party Telemetry**: Zero analytics, tracking pixels, or third-party advertising frameworks.
- **Internet Permission**: Required solely for initial application asset download and PWA service worker updates; all ledger operations work completely offline.
